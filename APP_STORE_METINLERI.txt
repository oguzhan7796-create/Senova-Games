【Description】 (max 4000)

BlockMir is a modern block puzzle game: drag colorful blocks onto the board, fill rows and columns, and clear them. With a 100-level adventure path, daily rewards, achievements, and rich customization, it is perfect for quick breaks and longer sessions.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Drag a block from the tray onto the board
2. A full row or column clears automatically
3. Chain clears for combos, bonus score, and Block Coins
4. The run ends when no valid moves remain

CLASSIC MODE
• 8x8 Quick Game — Tight board, fast pace, classic BlockMir feel
• 10x10 Wide Board — Longer runs, higher scores, deeper strategy
• Separate high scores and resume where you left off

ADVENTURE — 100 LEVELS, 10 WORLDS
From Mir Garden to Mir Summit across ten themed chapters with unique board themes, block skins, and clear effects.

Mission types include Score Hunt, Line Operation, Chain Master, Burst Wave, Strategy Route, Master Trial, and One Chance (no undo). Earn 1–3 stars per level and replay for better stars.

STAR ROAD
Unlock rewards with adventure stars: Block Coins, premium themes, block skins, clear effects, and the Mir Legend Set at 300 stars.

DAILY REWARDS
Log in daily for Block Coins. A 7-day streak grows your reward. Bonus hints from day 3, bonus undos from day 5.

22 ACHIEVEMENTS
Score, combo, clear, adventure, and collection goals — each pays Block Coins.

STATS AND BADGES
Profile, XP bar, separate 8x8/10x10 records, adventure star total, best combo, and 7 badges.

LEVEL AND XP
Earn XP from play and achievements. Bonus Block Coins every 20 player levels.

BLOCK MARKET
17+ board themes, 26+ block skins, 29+ clear effects, and power packs.

CUSTOMIZATION
Preset themes or your own photo from the gallery. Colorblind-friendly mode with shape markers on blocks.

IN-GAME TOOLS
Smart hints, undo, and combo scoring.

SETTINGS
Music, sound, vibration, graphics quality, Turkish/English.

OTHER
Offline play, ad-free experience, data stored only on your device.

BlockMir has no real money, betting, gambling, or cash prizes. Block Coins are for in-game progression and cosmetics only.
